The Skitch name arrived in Western Australia aboard the lives of Cornish emigrants. What followed was five generations of foresters and farmers, singers and servicemen, ordinary people living through extraordinary times. These documents are an attempt to preserve what might otherwise be forgotten.

## Robert Francis Skitch, 1934 to 2026

The heart of the collection is Bob Skitch, known to his family as Junior. Between 1999 and 2018 he wrote close to a million words about his own life, from memory: a Depression-era boyhood in the coalfields at Collie, National Service as a stoker in the Royal Australian Navy, twenty-six years in the Royal Australian Survey Corps including a tour in Vietnam, and nineteen years in the Queensland public service.

He died on 8 July 2026, aged 92. Wendy, his wife of more than sixty years, died nine months earlier.

He wrote nothing further after 2018, so the memoirs are closed. Not all of them have been digitised yet. The archive currently holds over 630,000 words across fifteen documents, and volumes are still being added, along with photographs, service records and family papers.

## How this archive is made

Each memoir is published in three synchronised forms, all generated from a single source document:

- A **web edition** here, with the original photographs and house-style typography.
- A **narrated edition**, the document read aloud with word-level highlighting, so it can be listened to rather than read.
- A **NotebookLM-safe edition**, a Google Doc prepared for [Gemini Notebook](https://notebook.google.com/), so the collection can be explored conversationally.

### Why the illustrations are sketches

In the NotebookLM edition, every photograph showing a person is replaced by a drawn sketch, with the caption unchanged.

This is not an editorial or stylistic choice, and it is not about privacy. NotebookLM removes images containing identifiable people during ingestion, so photographs of Bob and his family would simply be absent from that edition. The sketches survive ingestion where the photographs do not. Each caption links back to the original photograph here.

### Ask the archive a question

The public collection is at **[Mapping a Life: Robert F. Skitch](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/3a3bfc7e-fd73-49c6-a7e1-c4863af87c59)**. It holds the memoirs, an analytical report for each, and reference tables of people, places and chronology. It also contains twelve podcast episodes, one per memoir, running to about nine hours in total.

## The tools

Both are open source, MIT licensed, and were built for this archive:

- **[Notebook Forge](https://github.com/hold-shift/notebook-forge)** for ingestion, editing and publishing to all three targets.
- **[Forge Narrator](https://github.com/hold-shift/forge-narrator)** for the narrated editions.

## A note on this repository

The contents of this repository are generated output rather than hand-authored files. Everything here is written by Notebook Forge from source documents held elsewhere, so edits made directly to the published files will be overwritten on the next publish. Corrections to the memoirs themselves are very welcome: please open an issue rather than a pull request.
